Know More About AgriSetu

AgriSetu is a smart web-based Agricultural Subsidy Management System developed to simplify the registration, verification, monitoring and distribution of government subsidies to farmers.

The portal enables agricultural officers to digitally manage farmer records, monitor subsidy distribution, maintain inventory, visualize farmer locations through GIS mapping and generate comprehensive reports for better decision making.
